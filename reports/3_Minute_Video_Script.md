# 🎬 3-Minute Hackathon Presentation Script
## "Decoding Customer Experience on Brazil's Largest E-Commerce Marketplace"
**Team Hackangers | Gradient Learnings Data Analytics Hackathon**

---

> **Total Runtime:** 3:00 | **Slide Count:** ~8–10 | **Presenter Style:** Confident, data-first, business-focused

---

## [0:00 – 0:30] — THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

**[Slide: Map of Brazil + Olist logo + headline stat]**

*"Imagine you're running Brazil's largest e-commerce marketplace. You have 99,000 orders. 3,000 sellers. Customers in every corner of the country."*

*"And yet — 1 in 7 of your customers gives you a 1 or 2-star review."*

*"Why? That's exactly the question we set out to answer."*

*"Hi, we're Team Hackangers. Our project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand what drives poor customer experience — and what the business can do about it."*

---

## [0:30 – 1:00] — DATASET & APPROACH

**[Slide: Dataset diagram — 9 tables, 99K orders, schema overview]**

*"We worked with 9 relational datasets — nearly 100,000 orders, 3,095 sellers, and over a million geographic data points — covering orders placed between 2016 and 2018."*

*"Our approach was simple but disciplined: every analysis started with a business question, not a chart."*

*"We cleaned the data carefully — handling multi-item orders, multiple payments per order, date conversions, category translations, and geographic deduplication. The result: a clean, analytical master dataset built for honest insights."*

*"Total delivered revenue analyzed: over R$15.8 million."*

---

## [1:00 – 2:00] — THE KEY INSIGHTS

**[Slide 1: Delivery Status vs Review Score bar chart]**

*"Here's the most important finding in our entire analysis."*

*"When an order arrives EARLY — customers rate it 4.28 out of 5."*
*"When it arrives LATE — that score drops to just 2.26."*

*"A two-point collapse. That's not a correlation. That's a crisis signal."*

**[Slide 2: Root cause comparison table]**

*"And when we looked at 1-2 star reviews specifically — 32% of them involved a late delivery. Among 4-5 star orders, late delivery was present in less than 3%."*

*"That's an 11x difference."*

**[Slide 3: State map / bar chart — RJ late rate]**

*"Geographically, Rio de Janeiro stands out. It has 12,350 orders — that's 12% of our entire dataset — but a 12.1% late delivery rate. Nearly three times the São Paulo rate."*

**[Slide 4: Retention pie chart]**

*"And here's the number that should keep Olist's leadership up at night:"*

*"96.9% of customers NEVER come back for a second purchase."*

*"The platform is running on new customer acquisition alone. Every bad experience permanently removes a customer from the ecosystem."*

**[Slide 5: Category satisfaction chart]**

*"At the category level, office furniture averages just 3.48 out of 5. Audio equipment has an 11.5% late delivery rate. These aren't edge cases — they're patterns that need intervention."*

---

## [2:00 – 2:40] — BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

**[Slide: Priority recommendation cards]**

*"So what should Olist do? We ranked eight recommendations by evidence and implementation effort."*

*"Priority one: Build a Late Delivery Early-Warning System. Flag orders at risk by Day 5 post-purchase. Trigger carrier escalation. Send proactive customer communication. The data shows early intervention can soften the review score impact of delays."*

*"Priority two: Seller Accountability Scorecards. We identified roughly 45 at-risk sellers — defined as below 3.5 stars OR above 20% late delivery rate. These sellers need a formal Performance Improvement Program with defined consequences."*

*"Priority three: Fix the Rio de Janeiro problem specifically. Audit carriers. Add redundancy. Negotiate SLAs. 12,000 customers a period shouldn't face a 12% chance of late delivery."*

*"And priority four — the often-overlooked one: launch a post-delivery retention campaign. Customers who rate you 4 or 5 stars are your best asset. Reach them within 14 days. Give them a reason to come back. Even a 3% improvement in repeat purchase rate adds thousands of incremental orders at near-zero cost."*

---

## [2:40 – 3:00] — CONCLUSION & IMPACT

**[Slide: Executive dashboard / final summary]**

*"To summarize: Olist has a healthy marketplace, strong growth, and a 4.07 average review score."*

*"But beneath that headline number is a delivery-driven satisfaction problem, a geographic concentration of risk in RJ, a retention rate that should be a company-wide emergency, and category-level quality gaps that are dragging the platform's reputation."*

*"The good news: the data tells us exactly where to look, and exactly what to fix."*

*"Fix delivery reliability. Hold sellers accountable. Retain the customers you've already won."*

*"Do those three things — and Olist doesn't just improve its review score. It builds a marketplace that customers return to."*

*"Thank you. We're Team Hackangers."*

---

## Presenter Notes

- Speak at a confident, measured pace — approximately 130 words per minute
- On the delivery stat slides, pause briefly after the numbers for impact
- Show charts on screen while speaking — don't read them verbatim
- Make eye contact (or camera contact) during recommendations — these are your strongest moments
- Keep energy up in the final 30 seconds — end strong
